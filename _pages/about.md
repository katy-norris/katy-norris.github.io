Welcome! I am a final-year PhD Candidate in the Department of Political Science at University College London. My research focuses on the interplay between politics and education. My dissertation examines the politics of higher education, seeking to understand what motivates politicians to expand and contract access to higher education. This research draws on insights from original archival work across universities in the U.S.

Beyond my dissertation, I am involved in several research projects. These projects explore what motivates politicians to implement reforms to improve education quality, how teacher unions mobilize women to participate in politics, and how the experience of war shapes the quality of democratic institutions.
   
Before joining UCL, I worked in education policy and international development. I also completed an MPP in Inequality and Social Policy from the University of California, San Diego and an MSc in Development Studies from the London School of Economics & Political Science.
